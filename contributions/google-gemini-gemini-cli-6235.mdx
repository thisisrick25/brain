## What was done
- Refactored the content consolidation logic within the `GeminiChat` class for streaming responses.
- Modified text appending to combine new text with the *last* text part of existing content, instead of the first, to ensure correct sequence.
- Introduced and refined `isFirstPartText` and `isLastPartText` helper methods to accurately identify pure text parts within multi-part content.
- Enhanced these helper methods to explicitly validate for `model` role content and exclude non-text types such as `functionCall`, `functionResponse`, `inlineData`, `fileData`, and `thought` when checking for text parts.

## Impact
- Ensures accurate and correct concatenation of text within multi-part streaming responses from the Gemini model.
- Prevents garbled or improperly merged text content, improving the reliability and readability of AI-generated output.
- Developers working with `GeminiChat` will experience more robust content handling, especially in mixed-content streaming scenarios.

## Technical details
- Changes are implemented within the `packages/core/src/core/geminiChat.ts` file.
- The `consolidate` method in `GeminiChat` now uses the updated helper methods for precise text merging.
- The new logic uses robust type guards to differentiate between various `Part` types, ensuring only actual text parts are consolidated.
