## What was done
- Refactored the content consolidation logic in `GeminiChat` to correctly handle multi-part streaming responses.
- Changed text appending from the first part of existing content to the last text part.
- Introduced `isFirstPartText` and `isLastPartText` helper methods to precisely check if the first or last content part is pure text.
- Enhanced `isLastPartText` to validate only model-generated content and explicitly exclude non-text content types like `functionCall`, `inlineData`, or `thought`.
- Updated `isFirstPartText` to also explicitly exclude non-text content types for accurate identification.

## Impact
- Resolves incorrect concatenation of text within multi-part streaming outputs from the Gemini model.
- Improves the reliability and accuracy of text merging, especially when responses contain mixed content types (e.g., text interwoven with images or tool calls).
- Prevents text from being erroneously merged into non-text content parts.
- No breaking changes are introduced.

## Technical details
- Modifications were made in `packages/core/src/core/geminiChat.ts`.
- The core logic update involves changing how `consolidatedOutputContents` and `lastHistoryEntry` are modified to append text to `parts[parts.length - 1]` instead of `parts[0]`.
- New type predicate functions `isFirstPartText` and `isLastPartText` were added and refined to ensure strict validation of pure text parts, filtering out other content types.
